Platform Features

Everything your institution needs

From the first client registration to the final BNR submission Innoti handles the complete loan lifecycle.

👥

Full Client Registry

Register clients with National ID, employment, monthly income, and complete Rwanda address down to village level.

💳

Loan Engine

Supports flat rate and reducing balance. Live calculator shows interest, monthly payment, and total repayable before approving.

📅

Repayment Schedules

Auto-generated month-by-month schedule for every loan. Record payments and the balance updates instantly.

📊

BNR Reports

Monthly, quarterly, semestrial, or annual. PDF and Excel export. PAR ratio calculated automatically.

📂

Excel Import

Upload your existing Excel client data. Innoti reads it, previews it, and lets you analyze before importing.

🔒

Role-based Access

Platform owner, institution admin, and loan officer roles. Each sees only what they need to.

How It Works

Up and running fast

1

Institution onboarded

Innovatoor registers your bank or SACCO on the platform. Your data is fully isolated from other institutions.

2

Import existing data

Upload your current client list from Excel. No manual re-entry Innoti reads your file directly.

3

Issue & track loans

Create loans with auto-calculated schedules. Staff record payments as they come in.

4

Generate BNR reports

Select the period, click export — PDF or Excel is ready to submit to the National Bank of Rwanda.
